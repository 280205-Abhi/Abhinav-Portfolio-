Hotel Booking Cancellation & Revenue EDA

119k Records

Unchecked cancellations distorted hotel revenue projections and created undetected revenue leakage.

  • Cleaned 119,390 raw booking records, dropping 31,994 duplicate rows (~27%) first and repairing flawed date datatypes to secure data integrity.
  • Calculated genuine revenue exclusively from non-cancelled bookings, establishing a distinct metric to track revenue lost directly to cancellations.
  • Discovered the non-refundable deposit cancellation paradox where high-lead-time bookings suffered higher cancellation rates due to rigid non-refundable policies.
Key Result: Identified $22.97M actual revenue earned (calculated from non-cancelled bookings only) vs. $11.47M lost to cancellations, generating 6 actionable pricing and cancellation policy recommendations.
